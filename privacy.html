Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy explains how PixelSqueeze handles your data — or more accurately, why it can't.

The short version: PixelSqueeze processes all images entirely in your browser using JavaScript. No image data, file metadata, or personal information is ever transmitted to any server. We have no ability to see, store, or access your images.

1. Image Processing — Browser-Only

PixelSqueeze is a client-side-only web application. All image compression, conversion, and resizing is performed using browser APIs including the Canvas API, createImageBitmap(), OffscreenCanvas, and Web Workers. Your image files are:

  • Decoded in memory within your browser tab
  • Processed on your CPU without network access
  • Made available to you as a download via a temporary blob:// URL
  • Automatically garbage-collected when the tab is closed or files are removed

We cannot receive, store, or access your images because we have no server-side image processing infrastructure. This is not a policy that could change — it is an architectural fact enforced by the browser's security model.

2. LocalStorage — Theme Preference

PixelSqueeze stores exactly one item in your browser's localStorage: your dark/light theme preference under the key ps-theme. This is a string value ("light" or "dark") stored locally on your device. It is never read by our servers, never included in any analytics, and is deleted when you clear your browser's local storage. To remove it manually, open your browser's DevTools (F12) → Application → Local Storage → pixelsqueeze.site → delete the ps-theme entry.

3. Google AdSense and Cookies

Google AdSense code is installed on this site. When a page loads, the script may send requests to Google and its advertising partners. Cookies or similar technologies may be used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, or personalization, depending on visitor settings and applicable consent requirements. Read Google Privacy Policy.

When AdSense is active, information processed by Google may include:

  • Your browser and device type
  • Your approximate geographic location (country/city level)
  • Websites you have visited (for interest-based ad targeting)
  • Ad interactions (clicks, impressions)

AdSense does not have access to the images you process with PixelSqueeze.

4. Ad Personalization Opt-Out

If personalized ads are shown, you can manage them through Google and the following opt-out services:

  • Google Ad Settings — manage what data Google uses to show you ads
  • NAI Opt-Out Tool — opt out of interest-based advertising from NAI member companies
  • DAA Opt-Out Tool — Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out
  • Browser settings: most modern browsers allow you to block third-party cookies entirely under Settings → Privacy → Cookies

5. Third-Party Resources

PixelSqueeze loads the following third-party resources:

  • Inter typeface — served from this site’s own /fonts/ folder. No request is made to Google Fonts for typography.
  • JSZip (self-hosted) — a JavaScript library for ZIP creation, served by this website. No image data is transmitted to a library CDN.
  • UPNG.js (self-hosted) — a PNG quantization library. This library is served by this website; processing remains in your browser.
  • Google AdSense (googlesyndication.com) The installed script may make requests to Google and its partners when a page loads.

6. Analytics

PixelSqueeze does not currently use Google Analytics or any other analytics platform that tracks individual user behavior. If analytics are added in the future, this policy will be updated and a clear notice will be added to the site before implementation.

7. GDPR — Rights for EU/EEA Residents

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right to access: You can request what personal data we hold about you. Since we hold none, the response will always be "none."
  • Right to erasure: Your localStorage preference can be deleted at any time (see Section 2). Your browser's local data is entirely under your control.
  • Right to object: You can opt out of Google's personalized advertising as described in Section 4.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: You may contact your national data protection authority if you believe your rights have been violated.

In the current configuration, fonts and processing libraries are self-hosted and do not make third-party CDN requests. The contact page provides a mailto link. If you choose to send a message, your email is sent through your email provider to the address shown on the page. If advertising is enabled later, the site must update this notice and apply any consent requirements that apply to the visitor.

8. CCPA — Rights for California Residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected (we collect none about you directly)
  • Request deletion of personal information (none exists on our servers)
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising CCPA rights

For Google AdSense CCPA compliance, see Google's CCPA resource page.

9. Children's Privacy

PixelSqueeze is not directed at children under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 (GDPR). We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us so we can address it.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially (e.g., if analytics are added), we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and post a notice on the homepage for 30 days.
